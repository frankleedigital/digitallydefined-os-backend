# Reputation Intelligence Agent

**Execution Layer**: Buzz Agents  
**Dependencies**: OmniRoute  
**Purpose**: Analyze digital footprint and generate reputation improvement plan

## Role

This agent evaluates a user's social profiles, website, and content samples to assess their digital reputation and provide actionable improvement steps. Designed for users who want to build a professional, faceless online presence.

## Integration Points

- **Tools Page**: Will be featured as a future tool in the Tools section
- **Onboarding Flow**: Part of the complete DigitallyDefined onboarding sequence
- **Dashboard**: Can be integrated into the dashboard's profile/reputation module

## Tone & Style

- No fear-mongering or scare tactics
- Practical, actionable insights
- Faceless-by-design focus (building reputation without being on camera)
- No "reputation management" hype
- Direct, Gen X appropriate tone
