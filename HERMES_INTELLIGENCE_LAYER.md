# HERMES_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md

## Phase 1: Current State
Hermes receives: quiz results (personas), roadmap entries, product generator output, website editor content, Notion sync data, agent tasks. User data: quiz answers, emails, roadmap usage. Feedback: minimal (no structured feedback DB). Demand signals: none automated. Monetization: Gumroad links, no revenue tracking automation.

## Phase 2: Customer Intelligence Databases (Notion)
Customer Feedback, Feature Requests, Product Requests, Demand Signals, Opportunities, Hermes Insights — schemas as specified.

## Phase 3: Feedback Collection
Add feedback prompts to Quiz, Roadmaps, Products, Templates, Dashboard, Notion OS, Intelligence Package, AI Business Partner. Store in Customer Feedback.

## Phase 4: Demand Detection
Demand Score / Opportunity Score / Recommendation Score computed from frequency of requests + usage. Thresholds: 30+ requests = high demand.

## Phase 5: Product Opportunity Engine
Auto-generate Opportunity records with name, reason, evidence, target customer, suggested price, revenue potential, priority score. Types: template, mini-dashboard, lead magnet, workbook, prompt pack, calculator, planner, roadmap package.

## Phase 6: Notion Architect Integration
Strong opportunities (score >= 80) auto-create Build Request with product concept, audience, required DBs, features, source signals. Stored in Opportunities/Build Requests.

## Phase 7: Continuous Learning
Hermes reviews insights weekly, updates scores, archives stale opportunities, promotes high-demand ones to product packager.
