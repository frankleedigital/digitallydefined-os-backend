# Content Repurposer Agent

**Execution Layer**: Buzz Agents  
**Dependencies**: OmniRoute  
**Purpose**: Transform one piece of content into multiple formats

## Role

This agent takes a single piece of content (blog post, video transcript, article, etc.) and transforms it into multiple formats including email, blog posts, social media content, and scripts.

## Integration Points

- **Tools Page**: Available as a free tool on the website
- **Content Engine**: Part of the AI Content Engines card in the What You Build section
- **SEO Pillar Pages**: Used to repurpose pillar content into lead magnets and social posts

## Tone & Style

- No marketing fluff
- Practical content transformation
- Faceless-by-design (automated content repurposing)
- Consistent brand voice across all formats
