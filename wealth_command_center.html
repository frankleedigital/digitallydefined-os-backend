Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the crypto data come from?

All cryptocurrency prices are pulled live from the CoinGecko API. Prices update every 60 seconds on the dashboard.

How do I connect Supabase?

Go to Settings → Backend → enter your Supabase URL and Anon Key, then click "Connect". Your data will sync automatically.

How do I switch themes?

Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner, or go to Settings → Appearance. Choose Light, Dark, or Auto.

What is the Freedom Number?

Monthly passive income needed to replace your salary. Combines traditional dividends + digital MRR.

Is my data secure?

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Row Level Security ensures you only see your own data. No social features, no sharing, no ads.

How do Life Scenarios work?

Stress-test retirement against real events: parent care, grad school, layoff, health. Shows impact on retirement date + recovery strategies.
