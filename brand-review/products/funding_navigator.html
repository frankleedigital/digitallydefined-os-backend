The Objection Script (calm, not salesy)

1. "You're not a digital person."

"I wasn't. I built this with AI and 20 hours of learning. That's the point — I'm proof it's doable."

2. "Where's your traction?"

"Here: [one real number — list size, pre-sell, a completed asset, a customer]."

3. "Can you scale?"

"The asset is digital. Marginal cost of one more copy is near zero. Scaling is [one sentence]."

4. "Why you?"

"Because I've lived the gap I'm filling. I'm not selling a theory. I'm selling the thing that got me across it."

5. "What if this doesn't work?"

"Then I've lost [X] to learn it the way I was built to learn — through experiment. The downside is bounded. The downside of not trying is the 1% gap this generation is stuck in."
