How to Use This Vault.

Five rules. No shortcuts. The output is only as sharp as the input.

01

Add Your Specifics

Replace every [bracket] with real detail. Vague in = vague out. The more you fill, the sharper the output.

02

Add Examples

Paste a sample of your writing so the model mirrors your voice, not generic marketing-speak.

03

Push Back

If the first answer is generic, say "go deeper" or "give me the non-obvious version." Demand rigor.

04

Chain Prompts

Use the output of one prompt as the input to the next. These are designed to stack and compound.

05

Force Reasoning

Add "think step-by-step" to any prompt where you want analytical depth, not fast fluff.
