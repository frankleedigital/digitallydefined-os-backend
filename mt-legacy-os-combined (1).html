02B / Benefit Paths

The Three Survival Rails

This is the government benefits guide, rebuilt as a working product instead of a PDF. Each path gets mapped to eligibility logic, not just good intentions.

Medicaid Self-Directed Care

Available in all 50 states + DC. The care recipient chooses who provides care — including family — and Medicaid may reimburse that person’s wages. Typical range: $10–$25/hr, with state and program variation.

State Specific Waitlists Possible Relationship Rules

VA Family Caregiver Program

For caregivers of qualifying veterans. Stipend range: $1,800–$2,900/month. Requirements include disability rating, VA enrollment, and continuous in-person care needs. Support line: 1-855-260-3274.

Stipend Path 90-Day Target Assessment Required

Paid Family Leave

Operational in 15 states as of 2026. This is wage replacement during leave from your own job, not payment for caregiving itself. It has caps, waiting periods, and coverage gaps for hourly or self-employed workers.

Wage Replacement State Matrix Needed Not Care Payment

The Eligibility Chain

Many caregivers fail here: they file for their own distress relief before they establish care-recipient eligibility. The system to use is always this order: recipient eligibility first, then caregiver compensation second.

Critical Logic Order Matters
