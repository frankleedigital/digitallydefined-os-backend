## 2. Core Operational Flows

### A. Lead Generation Flow (100% Automated)
1. Social post generated by Gemini dispatches to Facebook/Instagram 3x daily.
2. Visitor clicks `https://digitallydefined.store/quiz`.
3. Visitor completes 9 situational questions.
4. Results scored instantly on-device in ~15ms (Zero drop-off).
5. Lead captured in Supabase `quiz_results` → Brevo sends persona roadmap.
6. Primary CTA points visitor into `https://digitallydefined.store/financial-hub` or `https://legacyos.digitallydefined.store`.

### B. High-Ticket Sales Flow ($297 Legacy OS)
1. Customer lands on `https://legacyos.digitallydefined.store`.
2. Clean, high-converting Soft Brutalist sales copy presents the 3-pillar system (Plan · Protect · Operate).
3. Customer clicks checkout → routed securely to `https://shop.digitallydefined.store/l/legacy_os_bundle`.
4. Gumroad handles payment, local tax calculation, and delivers `Legacy_OS_Bundle.zip` immediately.

### C. Founder Business Intelligence Flow
1. Every morning at 12:30 UTC, Vercel triggers Gemini Flash to summarize the previous 24 hours of quiz leads, conversions, and system alerts.
2. Executive brief lands in founder's Brevo inbox.
3. Founder opens private dashboard to review strategic product suggestions or execute moves in 1-click.
