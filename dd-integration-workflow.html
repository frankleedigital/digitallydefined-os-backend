Layer 04

Phased Build Sequence

The system does not ship all at once. Each phase is a sellable unit. The next phase is the upsell.

Phase 1

MVP

What ships: The combined Legacy OS page (what you already built). Three tabs: Plan, Protect, Operate. No backend. No SaaS account yet.

Financial Autonomy Planner (interactive sliders)
Caregiver's Survival Kit (benefit paths + exposure math)
Legacy Command Center (localStorage-based, no Firebase)
One-time purchase model: $47–297 depending on bundle

Phase 2

SaaS

What ships: The Legacy OS becomes a real SaaS. One account, one login, one dashboard. All three modules live inside the account.

Multi-user "Family Network" access (premium unlock)
Encrypted document vault (premium unlock)
One-Click Emergency PDF Export (premium unlock)
SMS notifications for critical tasks (premium unlock)
Recurring revenue: $29–79/mo

Phase 3

Growth

What ships: The affiliate layer activates. State-specific benefits matrix goes live. The Content Engine feeds the SaaS funnel at scale.

Home & Safety Audit module with vetted affiliate partners
Legal & Legacy Lifecycle tracker with outside integrations
State-specific Medicaid / PFL / SFC benefit matrices
Content Engine → SaaS lead generation loop
Affiliate revenue: 10–30% commission on partner products
Layer 05

Revenue Capture Flow

Where each dollar comes from, and when it arrives in the user's journey.

One-Time Sales (Phase 1)

Front-loaded. The user buys the system. The content engine drives the sale. No subscription yet.

Planner: $47–97
Survival Kit: $97–197
Command Center: $147–297
Full Stack Bundle: $297–497

Recurring SaaS (Phase 2)

Back-end. The user upgrades from one-time files to a live account. The platform becomes the operating system.

Free Tier: Single caregiver, one recipient, in-app notifications only
Premium Tier: Multi-user Family Network, encrypted vault, PDF export, SMS alerts
Pricing: $29/mo (single) · $79/mo (family)

Affiliate Layer (Phase 3)

Trust-gated. No affiliate links until the user has been a subscriber for 90 days. Then the engine recommends — it does not push.

Safety Audit: Vetted mobility / medical alert partners (10–20% commission)
Legal Module: Trust & Will / Rocket Lawyer integrations (15–30% commission)
Aide Referral: Care.com / local home-care networks (performance-based)
Disclosure: Always visible. "We may earn a commission from vetted partners."

Content Engine (All Phases)

Runs in parallel. The engine doesn't sell the product — it fills the funnel that sells the product. It's the distribution layer.

LinkedIn assets: Positioning content → planner leads
Facebook assets: Community content → survival kit leads
Instagram assets: Lifestyle content → command center leads
BYOK mode: User-generated content from their own API keys → viral loop
Layer 06

Guardrails

What this system is not. These boundaries protect the brand's authority and the user's trust.

Not Legal Advice

The Legal & Legacy module tracks milestones and connects to outside legal platforms. It does not draft, file, or interpret legal documents. Every user sees this disclaimer before reaching the module.

Not Medicaid Asset-Transfer Counsel

The benefits guide explains eligibility logic and application paths. It does not advise on asset transfers, the 5-year look-back rule, or penalty periods. Those conversations go to a state-specific elder-law attorney.

Affiliates Are Trust-Gated

No external merchant links in the first 90 days of a subscription. After that, recommendations appear as "vetted partner options" with visible commission disclosure. The premium tier is explicitly described as "less intrusive" than the free tier.

Single Caregiver First

The MVP is one caregiver, one care recipient, one shared calendar. Multi-user access is a Phase 2 premium feature. This keeps the build shippable and the first-user experience clean.
